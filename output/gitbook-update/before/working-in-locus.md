# Working in Locus

Conversations, Notebook, Browser, Git, Terminal, schedules, mobile access, and everyday controls.

Locus keeps the conversation central while project tools stay one click away.

* [Conversations & Context](/locus-docs/working-in-locus/conversations-and-context.md) covers workspaces, folders, search, Side Chat, transcript selection, attachments, outputs, context packs, Notebook, and memory.
* [The Inspector](/locus-docs/working-in-locus/the-inspector.md) explains Overview and every workspace panel.
* [Git Changes & Terminal](/locus-docs/working-in-locus/git-changes-and-console.md) covers diff review, branches, commands, and retained processes.
* [Browser & Dev Servers](/locus-docs/working-in-locus/browser-and-dev-servers.md) covers browser automation, real input, responsive tabs, Autofill, mobile emulation, and .locus/launch.json.
* [Mobile, Schedules & Background Work](/locus-docs/working-in-locus/mobile-schedules-and-background-work.md) covers the Activity Center, menu-bar operation, the companion, and managed services.
* [Shortcuts & Slash Commands](/locus-docs/working-in-locus/shortcuts-and-slash-commands.md) keeps the full keyboard and composer reference.

The right rail keeps Side Chat, Overview, Terminal, Browser, and Notes within reach. The overflow menu opens Changes, Files, Runs, AGENTS.md, Model Router, and Proxies. Notebook opens independently from the sidebar gear, Locus menu, command palette, or ⇧⌘9 so you can search every note without changing the current inspector panel.
