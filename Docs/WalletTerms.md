# Locus Vault terms — release candidate

These wallet-specific terms must receive counsel approval and an effective date
before public GA. They supplement the Locus application terms.

## Self-custody

Locus Vault is self-custodial. You control the recovery phrase and transactions.
SparkTales does not know the phrase, cannot reset it, cannot reverse a blockchain
transaction, and cannot restore access if your backup is lost.

## No advice or guaranteed outcome

Wallet balances, quotes, simulations, labels, and activity are informational.
They are not financial, investment, legal, or tax advice. Blockchain networks
and third-party protocols can fail, reorganize, upgrade, suspend, or return
incorrect data. A simulation or quote does not guarantee execution or price.

## Fees and third parties

You pay network gas/transaction fees and any fee displayed by a third-party
liquidity venue. Locus charges no swap fee in this release. RPC/indexer vendors,
wallet connection protocols, token projects, NFT media hosts, and swap venues
are independent services governed by their own terms.

## Supported authority

Locus supports only the networks, assets, calls, messages, and adapters shown in
the exact review screen and enabled by the signed release manifest. It does not
provide bridges, fiat rails, arbitrary signing, undecodable transactions,
cloud backup, hardware-wallet accounts, or a BIP-39 passphrase in this release.

## Eligibility and regions

Use is available only where the signed regional matrix enables the relevant
wallet capability and where you are legally permitted to use it. Do not evade
regional, sanctions, age, or protocol restrictions.

## Your responsibilities

Protect the recovery phrase, verify the raw destination and network, keep the
Mac secure and updated, review every exact confirmation and agent rule, start
with limited funds, and report unexpected signing immediately. Never send a
recovery phrase to support or a security researcher.

## Availability and liability

The wallet is provided on an as-available basis to the maximum extent permitted
by applicable law. Mandatory consumer rights are not excluded. The final
limitation-of-liability, governing-law, dispute, age, sanctions, and termination
clauses must be supplied and approved by counsel before this document becomes
effective.

Questions: use the repository support route. Security reports must follow
[the security policy](../.github/SECURITY.md).
